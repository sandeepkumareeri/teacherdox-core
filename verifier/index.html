Verify a teaching record

Check that a .tdx record — one class session, or a whole signed day — is authentic and has not been changed since it was signed. The check runs in your browser with the published public keys; nothing you paste is uploaded.

Public keys: loading…

Signatures are checked against this key registry. To check records from another issuer, paste its published registry.

The .tdx format, schema and verifier are open (Apache-2.0) — the same checks run from the command line with tdx verify file.json --keys keys.json. Part of TeacherDox Core.
